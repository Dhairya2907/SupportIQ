# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Standard delivery timelines
Orders are dispatched within 1 business day of payment confirmation. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days for metro cities and 5 to 8 business days for other pin codes. Express delivery, where available, takes 1 to 2 business days. Business days exclude Sundays and national holidays.

## Tracking your order
A tracking link is emailed and sent by SMS as soon as the courier scans the package. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first update after dispatch. If tracking has not updated for 48 hours, the customer can raise a ticket and the logistics team will contact the courier partner.

## Delayed delivery
If an order has not arrived within 2 business days after the promised date, the logistics team opens an investigation with the courier. Investigations are resolved within 3 business days. If the package cannot be located, the customer may choose a free replacement or a full refund.

## Package marked delivered but not received
Customers should first check with family members, neighbours, and building security, since couriers sometimes hand packages to them. If the package is still missing after 24 hours, raise a ticket. The team will obtain proof of delivery from the courier within 2 business days. If delivery cannot be proven, a replacement or refund is issued.

## Changing the delivery address
The delivery address can be changed free of charge until the order is dispatched. After dispatch, an address change within the same city can be requested through support and depends on the courier accepting the change. Changes to a different city are not possible after dispatch; the customer can refuse delivery and reorder.

## Failed delivery attempts
The courier makes three delivery attempts on consecutive business days. After the third failed attempt, the package returns to the warehouse and a refund is issued automatically within 5 to 7 business days. Customers can reschedule an attempt using the tracking link.

## Courier asking for extra payment
No additional cash is ever due on a prepaid order. For cash-on-delivery orders, only the amount shown in the order confirmation is payable. Customers should refuse to pay any extra amount and report the incident with the courier's name; such reports are escalated to the courier partner within 1 business day.
