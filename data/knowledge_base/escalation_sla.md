# Service Levels and Escalation

## First response targets
High priority tickets receive a first response within 2 business hours. Medium priority tickets within 8 business hours. Low priority tickets within 24 business hours. Business hours are 9:00 to 21:00 IST, Monday to Saturday.

## Resolution targets
High priority tickets are resolved or given a firm action plan within 1 business day. Medium priority within 3 business days. Low priority within 5 business days. Cases that depend on a courier or bank investigation follow the timelines in the shipping and billing policies.

## What counts as high priority
Suspected fraud or unauthorised account access, duplicate charges, customers locked out of their account, orders more than 5 days past the promised date, and any ticket where the customer has already contacted support twice without resolution.

## Escalation path
If a customer is unhappy with the handling of a ticket, they can ask for the ticket to be escalated. Escalated tickets are reviewed by a team lead within 4 business hours. A second escalation goes to the customer experience manager, who responds within 1 business day.

## Repeated contacts
When a customer contacts support more than once about the same issue, the ticket is merged, its priority is raised one level, and the same agent keeps ownership where possible so the customer does not have to repeat the history.

## Compensation
Agents may offer store credit up to Rs 500 for confirmed service failures such as missed delivery promises or incorrect information given by support. Higher amounts require team lead approval. Compensation is never offered pre-emptively or as a substitute for fixing the problem.

## Communication commitments
Customers receive an update whenever the ticket status changes and at least once every 2 business days while a ticket is open, even if there is no new information.
