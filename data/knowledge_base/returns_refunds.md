# Returns, Exchanges and Refunds Policy

## Return window
Most items can be returned within 10 days of delivery. Large appliances and furniture have a 7-day window. Innerwear, personal care items, software licences, and perishable goods are not returnable unless damaged or defective on arrival.

## Condition requirements
Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all tags, accessories, manuals, and the invoice. Electronics must not have been activated or registered. Items that fail inspection at pickup are returned to the customer and the return request is closed.

## Damaged or defective on arrival
Report damage or defects within 48 hours of delivery with photos or a short video. These cases qualify for a free replacement or full refund regardless of the item category, and pickup is prioritised within 2 business days.

## Wrong item or missing parts
If the wrong item, size, or colour was delivered, or accessories are missing, report within 48 hours. The correct item or missing parts are shipped at no cost, and the wrong item is collected at the same time where possible.

## Exchanges
Exchanges are available for a different size or colour of the same product, subject to stock. If the replacement is unavailable, the request converts to a refund. Only one exchange per order line is allowed.

## Return pickup
Pickup is scheduled within 2 business days of approving a return request. The courier makes two pickup attempts. If pickup fails twice because the item is not ready or no one is available, the customer must raise a new request. In areas without pickup service, the customer ships the item and the courier cost is reimbursed up to Rs 150 on submission of the receipt.

## Refund timelines
Refunds are initiated within 2 business days after the returned item passes quality inspection at the warehouse. The money reaches the customer's account in 5 to 7 business days for cards and net banking, 1 to 3 business days for UPI, and instantly to store credit. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded to a bank account or UPI ID provided by the customer.

## Refund amount
The refund equals the amount actually paid for the item, including any tax, after discounts and coupon adjustments. Shipping fees are refunded only when the return is due to damage, defect, or a wrong item. If a coupon applied to the whole order, the refund is proportionate.

## Returns outside the window
Returns requested after the return window are not accepted. Defects discovered later are covered by the manufacturer's warranty; support can share the service centre details.
