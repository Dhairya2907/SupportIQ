# Account and Login Policy

## Password reset
Customers can reset their password from the login page using Forgot Password. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive within 10 minutes, check the spam folder and confirm the registered email address. Support cannot see or set passwords.

## OTP not received
One-time passwords are sent by SMS and email and are valid for 10 minutes. If the OTP is not received, wait 60 seconds and request a new one. A maximum of 5 OTP requests are allowed per hour. Persistent OTP failures are usually caused by DND settings on the mobile number or an outdated number on the account; support can update the mobile number after identity verification.

## Account locked
Accounts are locked for 30 minutes after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. The lock lifts automatically. Repeated locks within 24 hours trigger a security review, and support may ask the customer to verify identity before unlocking.

## Suspicious activity or suspected hacking
If a customer reports a login they do not recognise, support immediately forces a logout of all devices and sends a password reset link. Any orders placed in the window of suspected unauthorised access are placed on hold and reviewed within 1 business day. Customers are advised to change the password of the associated email account as well.

## Changing registered email or phone number
The registered email or phone number can be changed in Account Settings after verifying both the old and new contact. If the old contact is no longer accessible, support can change it after identity verification using a recent order and the billing address.

## Two-factor authentication
Two-factor authentication can be enabled from Security Settings. If the authenticator device is lost, the backup codes shown at setup can be used to log in. Without backup codes, support disables two-factor authentication after identity verification, which takes up to 2 business days.

## Account deletion and data requests
Customers can request deletion of their account from Privacy Settings. Deletion completes within 30 days, after any open orders, returns, or refunds are closed. Transaction records required by law are retained for the statutory period. A copy of personal data can be requested the same way and is provided within 15 days.

## Identity verification
Identity verification requires the registered email or phone number plus either a recent order number or the full billing address on file. Support never asks for passwords, full card numbers, or OTPs.
