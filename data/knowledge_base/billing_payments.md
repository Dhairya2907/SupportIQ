# Billing and Payments Policy

## Duplicate or double charges
If a customer is charged twice for a single order, the duplicate payment is reversed automatically by the bank within 5 to 7 business days. If the amount has not returned after 7 business days, the customer should share the transaction reference numbers and the billing team will raise a chargeback with the payment gateway, which resolves within 10 business days.

## Payment deducted but order not placed
When money is deducted but no order confirmation is received, the bank reverses the amount automatically within 5 to 7 business days. The customer should not place a second order until the first status is confirmed. Support can check the gateway status within 1 business day.

## Invoices
A GST invoice is emailed when the order ships and is available for download from the Orders page. To receive an invoice with a business GST number, the number must be entered before placing the order; invoices cannot be edited after issue.

## Promo codes and discounts
A promo code must be applied before payment. Codes cannot be applied after an order is placed. If a valid code failed to apply because of a technical error, the customer can report it within 24 hours with a screenshot, and the discount is issued as store credit after verification.

## Price changes after purchase
Prices change frequently. Price differences after purchase are not refunded, except during a Price Protection event, which is announced on the offers page.

## Subscriptions and auto-renewal
Subscriptions such as the membership plan renew automatically on the billing date. Auto-renewal can be turned off from Account Settings at any time. Renewal charges can be refunded in full if cancellation is requested within 7 days of the renewal and no membership benefit was used in that period.

## EMI and payment options
EMI availability depends on the card issuer and cart value, with a minimum cart value of Rs 3,000. EMI conversion requests after payment must be made to the card issuer, not to the store.

## Currency and international cards
All prices are in Indian Rupees. International cards may be billed in the cardholder's home currency by the card issuer, and any conversion fee is charged by the issuer, not the store.
