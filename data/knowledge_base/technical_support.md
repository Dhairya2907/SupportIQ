# Technical Support Policy

## Mobile app problems
For crashes, blank screens, or features not loading, the first steps are: update the app to the latest version, clear the app cache, and restart the device. If the problem continues, the customer should share the device model, operating system version, and app version. Confirmed app bugs are forwarded to the engineering team, and the customer is notified when a fix is released.

## Website checkout or payment page errors
If the checkout or payment page fails, retry in a private browsing window or a different browser, and disable ad blockers. If an error code such as 500 or 503 appears, it is a server-side issue; the team monitors these and they are typically resolved within 2 hours. Customers should check their bank statement before retrying payment to avoid duplicate charges.

## Product setup and pairing
Smart devices must be set up on a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network; most do not support 5 GHz-only networks. Setup guides and videos are available on the product page. If pairing fails after following the guide, support arranges a call-back from a product specialist within 1 business day.

## Hardware faults within warranty
Products carry the manufacturer's warranty stated on the product page, typically 12 months. Faults discovered within 10 days of delivery are handled as a return or replacement under the returns policy. After 10 days, warranty service is provided through the manufacturer's authorised service centre; support shares the nearest centre and the warranty claim steps.

## Firmware and software updates
Firmware updates should not be interrupted. If a device stops working after an update, support first guides the customer through the manufacturer's recovery procedure. If recovery fails, the case is treated as a warranty claim.

## Response times for technical tickets
Technical tickets receive a first response within 8 business hours. Issues that prevent purchase or payment are treated as high priority with a first response within 2 business hours.

## Information to include in a technical ticket
Device or browser details, the exact error message or a screenshot, the steps that lead to the problem, and when it started. Tickets with this information are resolved roughly twice as fast.
